Fabrica

Fabrica is a fast-growing direct-to-consumer fashion brand focused on sustainable, made-to-order clothing. The client wanted to launch an AI-powered experience that allowed users to co-create their outfits
Client

Fabrica

Industry

Fashion Tech

Year

2024

Services Provided

UX Research & Strategy, AI Experience Design, Visual & Interaction Design

Key Challenges®

//04
Every innovation project brings its own set of complexities, especially when AI, user experience, and emerging technology intersect. In this project, our biggest challenge was to translate intelligent systems into human-centered interactions that felt natural, intuitive, and trustworthy.

Balancing Personalization with Simplicity

Users wanted flexibility in design, but not complexity.
//01

Fit Confidence

Incorrect sizing is the #1 reason for returns. We needed an AI layer that gave shoppers real confidence in how something would feel and look.
//02

Visualizing Infinite Combinations

With so many fabric/cut/style combos, we needed an efficient way to preview and recommend the most flattering options.
//03

Tone of AI

The assistant had to feel stylish, warm, and human — not robotic or generic.
//04

Design Approach®

//004
We crafted a layered AI experience — part stylist, part size expert, and part product selector — built around clarity, confidence, and creative control.

Smart Fit Assistant

A guided flow that lets users input preferences or use visual sliders to define body features (shoulder width, torso length, posture).
//01

Style Intent Mapping

We designed a language for users to express how they want to feel in the outfit: powerful, minimal, relaxed, bold, etc.
//02

Live Product Configurator

An interactive customization tool with 3D previews that render fabric drape, stitching, and motion.
//03
